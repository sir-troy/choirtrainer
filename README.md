# Choir Voice Trainer

Sing in tune, find your voice and read the grand staff.

**Live:** https://choirtrainer.useful-indeed.com

Choir Voice Trainer is a friendly practice partner for choir singers. It helps you find your voice type, warm up and breathe properly, sing notes in tune with live feedback from your microphone, read the grand staff using solfa (do-re-mi) and blend your part in S A T B harmony. Lessons are short steps you can do at home, and your progress is kept as a singing journey with stars.

## How to use

1. Pick your voice type — or let the trainer help you find it.
2. Turn on the microphone so the trainer can hear your pitch (you can also practise by ear).
3. Follow the short steps: warm-ups, breathing, singing notes, reading the staff and harmony.
4. Watch the live pitch line and try to stay in the green. Earn stars and continue your journey anytime.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your voice type, settings, stars and journey progress are saved in your browser's local storage so you can continue later. Microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded or stored.
