About Choir Voice Trainer

Sing in tune, find your voice and read the grand staff.

What it does

Choir Voice Trainer is a friendly practice partner for choir singers. A detailed test finds your voice type from your speaking voice and your lowest and highest notes, and notices when your voice switches to falsetto or strains. Then you warm up, breathe, sing notes in tune with live feedback, learn the Curwen hand signs, read the grand staff in solfa, blend in four-part harmony, and sing along with 30 songs or your own MIDI file. Finish every step to earn a certificate.

How to use it

  1. Pick your voice type — or let the trainer help you find it.
  2. Turn on the microphone so the trainer can hear your pitch (you can also practise by ear).
  3. Follow the short steps: warm-ups, breathing, singing notes, reading the staff and harmony.
  4. Watch the live pitch line and try to stay in the green. Earn stars and continue your journey anytime.

Why I made this

I don't trust most “free” tools online.

Most free websites that convert a PDF or crunch your numbers do it on their own servers. Your files and data leave your device, and you have to trust that they're deleted afterwards. Very few of them can honestly promise zero data retention.

I use these every day — so I made my own.

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Made for me, shared with you. Why not?

I built them for personal use, but there's no reason to keep them to myself. If they're useful to me, they might be useful to you too — free, no sign-up, and your data stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

Does it record my voice?

Your pitch is analysed live in your browser. The "Hear my blend" button keeps a short recording only on this page so you can listen back. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Is my name saved for the certificate?

No. You type your name only to show it on the certificate. It is never saved or sent anywhere, and the PDF is made in your browser.

Can I use my own songs?

Yes. In "Sing a song" you can upload a MIDI (.mid) file, choose the part you want to sing, and the other parts play along. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Which browsers work best?

Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari support the microphone and audio features. Use headphones if your speakers make the pitch line jumpy.

Do I need to read music already?

No. The lessons start from the basics and use solfa (do-re-mi) alongside note names.

Is my progress saved?

Your journey, stars and settings are saved in this browser only, so you can continue where you left off on the same device.

Your privacy, in short

Choir Voice Trainer does its work in your browser. Your voice type, settings, stars and journey progress are saved in your browser's local storage so you can continue later. Microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded or stored. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Choir Voice Trainer is a practice aid, not a substitute for a voice teacher. Stop if singing causes pain or strain.
